Pages

AimBot, Players, World, Skins, Misc and Config. The accent highlight slides to the active row and the pane fades in, exactly like the damped animation in ethnir_menu.h.

Search

Type esp to filter the row lists — the placeholder counts the functions on the page, and a keyword that matches nothing shows the “NO MATCHES” card.

Rows

Labels toggle switches, sliders drag on the accent track, combos open a menu that flips above the row near the pane’s bottom edge, colour chips cycle the palette.

Quick settings

The gear shows the floating panel: theme, animation speed, accent colour and the menu bind. Minimise collapses to the pill, Save flashes a confirmation.
