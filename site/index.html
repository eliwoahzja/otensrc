Try it

  • Tab switching. The accent pill slides down the sidebar gutter and the pane fades in, exactly like the damped animation in equinox_menu.h.
  • Search. Type esp to filter rows on VISUALS; type nomatch for the “NO MATCHES” card used by the filtering tabs.
  • Rows. Toggles flip, sliders drag, combos open a dropdown that flips above the row near the pane’s bottom edge.
  • Header. Drag the strip to move the window, tap the crosshair / eye / list / cog shortcuts, or use the traffic lights: red collapses to the floating pill, yellow jumps to VISUALS, green swaps the backdrop.
